About

I'm a PhD candidate in the Wang Lab at the Institute for Systems Genetics at NYU Langone Health. I build AI agents and models for small-molecule and protein design.

Before NYU Langone, I spent three years as a research associate in the Farhat Lab (Maha Farhat) at Harvard Medical School, where I built an AI agent for antibiotic discovery against Mycobacterium tuberculosis, one of the deadliest infectious diseases in the world. It designed five de-novo molecules that inhibited the bacterium in vitro, giving a drug pipeline that has produced few new antibiotics in decades a fresh set of candidates. Alongside that, I worked on clinical machine learning for hospital-onset infection and antimicrobial resistance at Harvard Pilgrim Health Care and Brigham and Women's Hospital, and on early-stage pandemic forecasting at Harvard Business School.

I hold an MS in Computational Biology and Quantitative Genetics from the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health and a BS in Biology and Computer Science from Wake Forest University.

Fleming: an AI agent for antibiotic discovery in Mycobacterium tuberculosis

★ First author

Harvard Medical School · 2022–2025

An AI agent that combines inhibition prediction, pharmacokinetic property prediction, diffusion- and structure-based molecule generation, and molecular optimization to identify molecules with high antibiotic potential against M. tuberculosis.

  • 83%in vitro hit rate for predicted inhibitors
  • 100%hit rate for de novo designed molecules

AI for Drug DiscoverybioRxiv · 2026

Active learning for narrow-spectrum Lyme disease antibiotics

Co-author

Harvard Medical School, with the Hu Lab at Tufts · 2022–2025

An active learning pipeline for high-throughput screening of chemical libraries against Borrelia burgdorferi, raising hit rates and speeding up discovery. Selective inhibitory molecules from the pipeline were tested in collaboration with the Hu Lab at Tufts.

  • 5×higher hit rate than investigator-selected screening plates
  • 53×hit-rate enrichment in the prospective virtual screen
  • 11%of predicted compounds confirmed to inhibit B. burgdorferi
  • Active learning
  • Drug discovery

AI for ClinicalNature Communications · 2026

Hospital-onset infection and antimicrobial resistance

★ Co-first author

Harvard Pilgrim Health Care · Brigham and Women's Hospital · 2022–2025

A matched case-control analysis of Mass General Brigham electronic health records: how colonization pressure from ward co-occupants relates to hospital acquisition of 11 drug-susceptible and drug-resistant organisms, with cases and controls matched on prior antibiotic exposure across 14 antibiotic classes.

  • 43,403patients from Mass General Brigham records, 2015–2024
  • 1.33×odds of acquiring C. difficile per unit colonization pressure
  • ~4×more hospital acquisitions of drug-susceptible than resistant organisms
  • Clinical ML
  • Causal inference

Co-author

Harvard Pilgrim Health Care Institute · four-center collaboration

Machine learning models that use electronic health record data to estimate, before culture results return, whether an antibiotic will be active against a patient's infecting bacteria, and to predict the organism and its resistance pattern, using microbiology data pooled from four U.S. academic medical centers.
